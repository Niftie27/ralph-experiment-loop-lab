# Project ledger

## Status legend

- OPEN — unresolved work.
- DONE — completed and verified.
- PAUSED — intentionally deferred.
- DECIDED — a recorded decision.
- INVALIDATED — superseded or disproven item; retain its ID.

Items come in part B from Claude's seed; do not invent items.

Ledger IDs never change; new items get the next free ID. No items are assigned in part A.
