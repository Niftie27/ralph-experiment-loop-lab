## Q1. C7 P0: unresolved evidence and the five-percent rule

**reasoning — input contract:** Needed inputs are frozen exact symbol/key identities; authenticated source bytes and source status; ordered pre-seal timestamps and interval tokens; month/segment membership including empty months; derived settlement hours and adjacency; observed/censored roles; era and TREE flags; pinned classifier identity; and dated documentation for any proposed amendment. Rates and prices are not inputs. Listing completeness, actual labels, missingness and TREE availability are not verified. Published fields are specified, derived fields are locally derivable, source availability is unknown, and the causal trigger remains unobservable without forbidden rate evidence. O0 alone is not the whole contract.

**reasoning:** Five percent is not an evidential confidence level. One unavailable month can contain every exit of a rare regime, while hundreds of unrelated regular months dilute its weight. A routing exclusion is particularly dangerous: it can hide any role. At exactly 5%, P0 does not trigger. Empty verified files contribute zero failures to that denominator. An unavailable TREE control is explicitly reported by S3 but is not a P0 reason; this is internally allowed by v2, yet weaker than my v1 unresolved-control recommendation. Likewise a parser-policy violation with no hourly role, unknown documented rule applicability, and unavailable context can survive the closed list. P3 tests existence, not representativeness or absence of correlated exclusions.

**reasoning — recommendation:** Set the source-failure tolerance for an unqualified frozen-census decision to **0%**, including empty listed research prefixes as unresolved. Continue collecting as F2/F3 allow; zero tolerance governs the decision, not an early acquisition stop. This is deliberately strict, not a claim that one irrelevant missing object invalidates every observed pair. A limited subset statement remains useful; any relaxation must be a new scoped decision, not an author-selected percentage after seeing labels. Absence from the frozen listing remains an explicit archive-universe limitation, not proof of historical nonexistence.

**reasoning — exact replacement P0:**

> P0 is evaluated first. The gate is inconclusive if the freeze is unresolved or empty; any frozen source is excluded, not verified, or has an empty pre-seal research prefix; the fixed TREE control is unavailable or partial; routing or required context remains unresolved; a required code identity or Node version differs; either independent computation disagrees; any eligible pair endpoint is outside the timing/interval parser contract; or required documented applicability for a proposed amendment is unresolved. No amendment or protected invocation is authorized by this outcome. Collection may finish under F2/F3. Report verified observations and every stop, but do not promote subset compatibility to census acceptance. Tomas may authorize a separately defined reduced claim; do not substitute a control or infer absence of an episode from missing evidence.

**reasoning:** An unavailable historical month not listed at all is distinguishable from a failed frozen object. Report all absent months and resulting segment breaks, but do not automatically declare every newly listed contract invalid. To claim an uncensored boundary requires an actual adjacent row; no absent month may be bridged. Even zero failures cannot exclude missing rows inside otherwise valid files or prove historical archive completeness.

## Q2. C7 P3: both outer directions in both later eras

**reasoning:** Too strict as a necessary condition for useful evidence, but not demonstrably “near certain”: occurrence probabilities are not verified. The May rule did not promise automatic returns; separate announced reversions were possible. Requiring an era2 exit as a universal gate requirement can reject a faithful sample. Also D's outer boundaries are cadence boundaries, not necessarily administrative transitions. Entry into hourly cadence usually has its trigger as a1, so the actual first hourly payment is the entry_inner pair. An exit to a slower grid with elapsed 1 h can be exit_inner. Four outer cells do not guarantee four administrative label transitions; one observation per cell does not establish completeness.

**reasoning — exact alternative P3:**

> P3 applies when either era2 or era3 has no eligible hourly-related pair, or when no retained run has an observed eligible outer boundary. Report entry_outer, entry_inner, exit_inner and exit_outer evidence separately by era, with zero and censored cells explicit. Do not require every direction in every era to occur. Any direction or documented regime absent from the evidence is outside the compatibility claim; an amendment needing that absent evidence requires a new decision and cannot authorize the protected invocation. Only isolated candidates, no observed boundaries, or evidence confined to era1 remain coverage outcomes requiring Tomas's decision.

**reasoning:** This retains the v1 non-vacuity floor without treating a structural zero as a data error. If Tomas instead wants both directions in both eras as an acceptance requirement, retaining v2 P3 is coherent; describe it as a deliberately stronger coverage decision, not a guaranteed attainable census property. Stops are reported even when coverage takes precedence.

**reasoning:** P5 does not hide a role-none stop from O5, but hides it from the decision. A hypothetical same-label 4-hour pair at 00:00 and 08:00, away from any detected run, is an unexplained_gap with role none. It might be missing data, an unobservable hourly episode, or another issue; none can be selected as its cause here. D4 correctly limits identifiability, but that limit is precisely why role none cannot establish irrelevance to a general classifier amendment. Nothing identifies which, if any, pattern occurred in HYPE 2026-06.

**reasoning — exact replacement P5 and related P1/P2 scope:**

> Report every eligible pair stop, including role none, without diagnosing it as an hourly event or an ordinary missing settlement. Role-none stops block a general compatibility claim and protected invocation until their disposition is frozen from documentation and pre-seal evidence. If any stop remains unexplained, the outcome is inconclusive and goes to Tomas. P1 requires all eligible pairs to pass, subject to P0/P3. P2 covers stops of any role, but authorizes an amendment proposal only under its existing generic-rule, documentation, all-pair validation and unchanged-interval-gap protections. A role-none stop alone is never permission to loosen the gap rule or inspect protected data. Preserve separate hourly-only findings as explicitly limited observations.

**reasoning:** The contract's m6 stops the F1 phase on every unexplained research gap/off-grid case. m9 is a separate external-symbol study, so P5 is not automatically a direct breach of m6; it is a dangerous weakening of what an m9 pass can justify. Keep that scope distinction rather than manufacturing a contract contradiction.

**reasoning:** O0 is an appropriate rate-free timing input. Reusing the same pinned classifier checks integration and aggregation, not whether the classifier itself is correct; independent synthetic expected classifications remain necessary. Recomputing from the same O0 cannot independently prove that the producer extracted every row correctly. The raw fixture subset can test extraction on selected files, not certify unsampled files.

**reasoning — leak paths:** O7 mandates raw funding archives; these contain the rate column and its values, directly conflicting with O's no-rate outputs. Selection every 50th key can include October, publishing sealed rows as well. An excluded fixture may have unreadable routing, so it cannot safely be declared pre-seal. The mandatory TREE months are pre-seal but still carry rates. S1's exact exclusions can protect the six only if keys are resolved under allowed external prefixes and no generic default downloader/reader is called. Importing the classifier is not itself a six-file read: the visible module's CLI call is guarded; dependency behavior still needs a frozen closure. Do not call parseFunding as the gate parser: it validates rates, restricts intervals and checks sealed timestamp order/month, unlike I2.

**reasoning — exact safe O7 replacement:**

> Do not commit or transfer raw archives. Keep source zip/CHECKSUM bytes in the isolated acquisition environment only. Publish rate-free pre-seal timing projections for the predeclared fixture keys, bound to source key and source sha256 and to the projection sha256. Use the same I2 routing before projecting; unavailable or excluded sources produce a status/reason record, never unsafe bytes. Preserve fixture selection before CSV inspection. An independently implemented extractor checks these selected local source bytes against the projections within that environment and emits only the allowed comparison result. Claude checks projected fixtures against O0; label this projected-data recomputation, not independent authentication of raw bytes in the sandbox. Use synthetic fixtures for malformed archives/routing and parser rejection cases. Any decision to export real raw fixtures requires a separate explicit change to the no-rate/no-sealed-row boundary before execution.

**reasoning — O0 size estimate, not measurement:** April 1 through October 16 inclusive has 199 days, or 4,776 hours. I2 permits at most one retained row per settlement hour, so a continuously covered symbol has at most 4,776 O0 rows. For 300–1,000 symbols this is at most 1,432,800–4,776,000 rows. At an assumed 30–60 bytes per output line, roughly 43–287 MB decimal (41–273 MiB). At 10,000 source keys, the looser per-key 744-row ceiling is 7,440,000 rows, about 223–446 MB at the same line-size assumption. These are planning bounds/scenarios, not observed counts or byte guarantees: identifier and integer token lengths are not bounded by v2. Mostly 4/8-hour schedules reduce rows, while CSV windows, pair outputs and matrices add space. Stream to disk; millions of JS row objects can exceed 512 MiB even when CSV bytes fit.
