**reasoning — exhaustive detection limitations by observable shape:**

- A singleton with neither adjacent step equal to one hour is absent from D. O2 catches it only if its published label is 1 and it is farther than three settlements from every detected run. A singleton without label 1 is not identifiable as hourly from these fields alone; no detector can certify an unrecorded administrative regime. An entirely absent episode cannot be detected. Joining kept files across an excluded month fabricates adjacency evidence, even when it does not create a one-hour step.
- Two administrative episodes separated by an unobserved switch, or an exit whose first four-hour-grid payment is just one hour after the last hourly payment, can form one uninterrupted D run. O2 excludes all its rows. D observes maximal one-hour cadence, not the exact first/last settlement governed by the hourly setting. Conversely, a label-1 singleton within three settlements of a run is suppressed by O2: O1 can contain that row while omitting one neighbor needed to classify its other incident pair. Distance-based deduplication is not pair coverage.
- An episode ending in delisting, at the seal, at the sample edge, or at a broken source boundary has no observed exit. Calling all such cases simply open confuses ongoing hourly settlement with missing evidence or termination. An administrative hourly regime with no archived payment is wholly unobservable here.
