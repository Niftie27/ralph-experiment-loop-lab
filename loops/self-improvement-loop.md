# Self-Improvement Loop

Purpose: improve RALPH's own research process.

Trigger:

- After repeated noisy sources.
- After a bad candidate.
- After a useful decision.
- Weekly or manually.

Actions:

1. Review recent log entries.
2. Identify stale unknowns and candidates.
3. Identify source types that produced signal or noise.
4. Update scoring rules, loop instructions, or templates.
5. Record the change in `log.md`.

## Automatic Improvement Rules

The loop may automatically improve the system when the change is internal and makes the research harder to fool:

1. Add or tighten verification checks.
2. Add data-quality flags.
3. Add low-sample gates.
4. Add shadow ledgers or scorecards.
5. Improve queue prioritization.
6. Improve Obsidian/wiki retrieval links.
7. Move repeated failures into discard memory.
8. Split broad tasks into smaller bounded queue items.

The loop must ask Tomas before:

1. Loosening a gate.
2. Promoting a candidate to paper/live.
3. Changing watcher wording, thresholds, sizing, risk, TP/SL, cron cadence, or execution.
4. Adding paid data, credentials, exchange accounts, wallet access, or new recurring automation.

Outputs:

- updated loop docs
- updated rules or templates
- updated source scoring notes
- updated Obsidian-ingested wiki notes when the improvement is durable

Safety:

- Do not loosen hard constraints without Tomas explicitly approving the exact rule change.
