# Decision Loop

Purpose: convert evidence into explicit decisions.

Inputs:

- candidates
- unknowns
- investigation briefs
- experiment results
- case files

Actions:

1. Review evidence.
2. Assign decision state: investigate, benchmark, prototype, watch, discard, defer.
3. Record rationale and caveats.
4. Define revisit trigger.
5. Update candidates and unknowns.

Outputs:

- updated `decisions/decisions.md`
- updated `decisions/candidates.md`
- updated `decisions/discarded.md` if needed
- decision memo under `outputs/decision-memos/`
