◆ ExternalCalculation

bool ATAS.Indicators.BaseIndicator.ExternalCalculation
getset

When true the indicator's own OnCalculate pipeline is suppressed and its data series are filled by an external calculation service (the platform's multi-timeframe projection host, PLAT-4527). The host listens to ExternalCalculateBarRequested / ExternalRecalculateRequested to keep the series in sync. Not persisted; set by the host only.
