CandleSeriesTimeFrame CalculationTimeFrame [get, set] 

The timeframe the indicator calculates on (PLAT-4527). Default is CandleSeriesTimeFrame.Chart - the chart's own series. Any other value makes the platform compute the indicator on that timeframe's series and project the values onto the chart bars as steps: history uses closed higher-timeframe bars only (no lookahead), the last chart bar tracks the forming higher-timeframe bar until it closes. Hidden from settings when BaseIndicator.DenyCalculationTimeFrameChange is set or MTF data is unavailable.
