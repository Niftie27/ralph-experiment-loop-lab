## Current Shape

RALPH has five active lanes:

1. Real-time alert observation.
2. Scheduled research/stat refresh.
3. Strategy destruction and validation.
4. Copytrading and wallet-following source research.
5. Orderflow data-rail validation, currently Binance historical aggTrades first; ATAS exports are manual examples unless Tomas provides matching historical/replay windows.

The center of gravity is now the `RALPH Profitability Flywheel`, formalized in `core/profitability-flywheel.md`: alert-edge and watcher outputs create paper evidence, autoresearch turns that evidence and prior art into explicit candidate ideas, and the strategy-destruction filter rejects weak ideas before any paper/demo promotion. Copytrading/wallet-following is a first-class source lane under `wiki/notes/2026-08-26-copytrading-autoresearch-lane.md`, but remains watch/shadow/paper-only. See `wiki/notes/2026-08-25-profitability-loop-integration-map.md` and `wiki/notes/2026-08-29-ralph-grand-loop-verify-reassess.md`.
