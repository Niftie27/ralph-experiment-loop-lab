# Overview

RALPH is a meta-workspace for crypto, DeFi, MEV, benchmarks, data rails, and possible trading-system components.

Its job is to save time by turning loose inputs into:

- durable source memory
- source summaries
- concepts, entities, and comparisons
- unknowns
- candidates
- experiments
- decisions
- discard records

RALPH starts from AI Research OS conventions, then adds a decision layer and automated research loops.

The current live direction is not Polymarket by default. After ingesting the wallet-shadowing transcripts and Tomas's corrections, the stronger candidate direction is now wallet-shadowing archetype research with forward paper-trade validation. Hyperliquid is the first practical data-feasibility branch, not the default alpha thesis. The central selection axis is whether an edge is slow enough to survive Tomas's detection, processing, execution, fee, and slippage costs. Trump means Trump as president/person and market-moving statements. TRUMP coin, MELANIA coin, and token treasury clusters are out of scope unless Tomas reopens that thread.

The current patient-retail constraint set is [[wiki/concepts/patient-retail-strategy-map]], sourced from [[wiki/sources/patient-retail-strategy-map-2026-07-01]]. It prioritizes smart-money accumulation cohorts and funding/basis structural baseline over latency-sensitive directional copying.

The current build stance is [[wiki/concepts/tool-first-not-build-first]], sourced from [[wiki/sources/tool-first-pivot-2026-07-01]]. RALPH should use existing tools before building custom infrastructure.

Primary sources are now ingested under `raw/` and summarized under `wiki/sources/`, including the RALPH transcript, Crypto Trading Bots handoff, MEV bot archive, Zela planner handoff, and AI Research OS docs.

The latest operating thesis says strategy discovery comes first. RALPH should research strategy families, trading bot frameworks, repo architectures, market-event triggers, simulations, and failure modes before proposing a full bot design.
