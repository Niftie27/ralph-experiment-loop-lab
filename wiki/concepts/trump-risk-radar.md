# Trump The Person Event Radar

This radar is about Donald Trump as president/person and his market-moving statements. It is not about TRUMP coin, MELANIA coin, or Trump-branded token treasury flows.

## What It Watches

- event-anomaly whale wallets around Trump-person statements;
- public statement windows: Truth Social posts, tariff announcements, crypto reserve comments, geopolitical remarks, scheduled appearances;
- unusual stablecoin movements;
- new large perp positions around scheduled public events or unexpected political posts;
- Polymarket/geopolitical anomaly leads only when they relate to Trump-person events.

## What It Does Not Do

- It does not assert that timing-based event whales are Trump wallets.
- It does not auto-copy event wallets.
- It does not track TRUMP/MELANIA coin material unless Tomas explicitly asks.
- It does not mix contracts, pool IDs, CEX deposit endpoints, and EOAs into one alert tier.

## Why It Matters

Trump-person statements can move BTC/ETH/SOL/XRP/ADA and broader market risk quickly. Event-anomaly wallets can be useful as sirens, but they are not a copy engine unless a separate validation path proves otherwise.

## RALPH Rule

Trump-person event radar outputs should be alerts, context, or risk notes. They should not become live entries without a separate strategy and validation path.
