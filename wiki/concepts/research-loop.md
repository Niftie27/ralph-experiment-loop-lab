# Research Loop

A RALPH loop is a bounded recurring process that reads sources, updates the wiki, creates unknowns or candidates, and stops before execution.

Every loop needs inputs, outputs, cadence, stop conditions, and safety boundaries.

The AI Research OS workshop video reinforces that loops should not only answer questions in chat. When a loop finds source-grade evidence or reusable synthesis, it should write back into the durable research directory so future agents can read index/wiki first.
