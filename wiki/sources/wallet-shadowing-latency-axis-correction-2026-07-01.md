## Executive Summary

The strongest correction is that wallet shadowing should be evaluated by edge speed relative to Tomas's latency, not by coin or famous wallet.

BTC and much of ETH are poor default directional shadow targets because price discovery is usually CEX-led and too fast. More shadowable edge likely exists in slower archetypes:

- structural/funding/basis wallets;
- mid-cap accumulation wallets;
- aggregate cohort flow signals;
- Hyperliquid perps as one technical branch, not the whole strategy.

## RALPH Interpretation

This source demotes "Hyperliquid-first" from default strategy to first data rail / technical branch. RALPH's strategy loop should now compare edge archetypes by latency tolerance before over-investing in any one implementation.
