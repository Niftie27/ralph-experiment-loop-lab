# Zela Planner Handoff

Raw source: [[raw/zela-planner-handoff]]

## Why This Source Matters

This source records the pivot after Zela shutdown: the benchmark no longer has a live target, and the project should become a methodology or builder-credibility artifact rather than an endless benchmark continuation.

## Key Claims

- Zela live target was confirmed dead; live smoke against Zela is permanently moot.
- Default strategic option was to ship a learnings/methodology artifact, not auto-continue the build.
- Any published numbers should be caveated as early beta / discontinued-product context.
- The important transferable lessons are methodology: paired async measurement, persistent-session bias, leader match analysis, multi-agent planner/builder/reviewer workflow, and caveat discipline.

## RALPH Implications

- Zela belongs under RALPH as benchmark discipline and case-file evidence.
- RALPH should not continue a benchmark unless the workflow under test is clear and worth the time.
- Public publishing remains forbidden in current RALPH unless Tomas explicitly approves it.
