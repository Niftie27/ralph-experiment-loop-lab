# Community Idea Kill-Test Template

## Purpose

This closes `investigation.community-idea-to-kill-test-template`.

The template converts public operator/profile/community ideas into falsifiable RALPH research records before they can influence strategy work. It is intentionally stricter than a source bookmark because Tomas wants the loop to learn from existing wheels without copying social claims or building from vibes.

## Output

Added `wiki/notes/templates/community-idea-kill-test.md`.

Required fields:

- source URL and surface;
- access classification;
- exact claim;
- mechanism;
- market fit and regime dependency;
- reuse path;
- data needed;
- dumb baseline;
- cheapest kill test;
- kill criteria;
- source quality;
- failure modes;
- bounded decision;
- next micro-action.

## Decision Rules

Allowed decisions from the template are only:

- `Rejected`;
- `Watch`;
- `Candidate`;
- `Blocked`;
- `Reassess`.

The template cannot produce `Paper-Qualified`, `Alert-Qualified`, live, execution, sizing, TP/SL, or strategy-promotion states. Those require later evidence gates under the Profitability Flywheel.

## Reassess

Next useful branch is a small GitHub/code-backed operator source map, not a broad X/Reddit scan. GitHub and docs are better first because source quality is easier to inspect and reproduce. X/Twitter and Reddit can still supply ideas, but every idea needs code/data/source-backed falsification before it becomes a candidate.

No live trading, alert wording, thresholds, risk, sizing, TP/SL, execution behavior, orders, accounts, keys, paid services, public posting, scheduler, cron, systemd, dependency adoption, watcher behavior, or strategy promotion changed.
