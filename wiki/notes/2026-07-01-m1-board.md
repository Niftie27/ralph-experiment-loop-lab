# RALPH M1 Board

M1 is the strategy discovery engine. The next work should reduce uncertainty about what kind of bot or system is worth building, not design execution yet.

## Current M1 Goal

Produce a ranked map of possible strategy families under Tomas's constraints:

- small capital
- roughly $100/month preferred infra budget
- no live trading
- no wallet keys
- no exchange accounts
- no paid infra without approval
- research/backtest/simulation before prototype

After ingesting the wallet-shadowing transcripts and Tomas's latency-axis correction, the current working strategy family is:

> Wallet-shadowing archetype research. Hyperliquid is the first practical data-feasibility branch, not the default alpha thesis.

The patient-retail strategy map adds a stronger constraint:

> Prefer branches that do not require latency competition and can tolerate hours-to-days entry delay.

The tool-first pivot adds another constraint:

> Do not build a bot, scanner, data collector, grid tool, or paper harness if an existing product or open-source tool already handles that job.

The current slow-accumulator tool check is [[wiki/sources/slow-accumulator-tool-fit-check-2026-07-01]].

The former "be the house" liquidation-map branch is [[wiki/sources/liquidation-map-liquidity-provision-2026-07-01]]. It is now closed for the tested Q1 configuration.

The feasibility spec is [[wiki/notes/2026-07-01-liquidation-map-feasibility-spec]].

The corrected prior-art gap map is [[wiki/sources/liquidation-map-prior-art-gap-map-2026-07-01]], but broad gap-map work should not continue after the Q1 kill unless explicitly reopened.

The corrected Q1/baseline kill switch is [[wiki/sources/liquidation-q1-baseline-kill-switch-2026-07-01]].

The liquidation-map Q1 branch has been killed for the June 2026 BTC/ETH/SOL replay window. See `experiments/liquidation-q1-baseline-kill-switch/results/major-2026-06.json` and [[wiki/notes/2026-07-02-strategy-leg-garden]].

The main question is now:

> Which edge is slow enough to be shadowable after Tomas's latency and costs?

Tomas's preferred near-term mode is exploratory: collect more strategy legs and notes until something clicks, rather than forcing an immediate choice between smart-money accumulation and funding/basis. RALPH should widen the leg surface while keeping each leg shaped for cheap falsification.

## Current Bias

Bias toward low-cost research rails:

- CEX/backtest first for cheap signal testing
- EVM simulation only if a strategy demands it
- wallet-shadow/event bot only after falsification
- prioritize slower edges over faster edges when shadowability is the question
- Hummingbot primarily as architecture reference unless capital fit is proven
- speed MEV likely discard for current budget
