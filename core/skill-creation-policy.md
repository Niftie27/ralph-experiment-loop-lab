# Skill Creation Policy

RALPH may propose reusable skills when repeated loop behavior teaches a durable method.

## When To Propose A Skill

- The same procedure succeeds at least twice.
- The same loop failure repeats at least three times.
- A new checklist prevents a meaningful mistake.
- A research workflow becomes stable enough to reuse.

## Examples

- MEV case-file audit skill.
- Trading repo triage skill.
- Wallet/entity rail evaluation skill.
- Candidate scoring skill.
- Market event sample extraction skill.

## Rules

- Skills are proposals first.
- Do not apply or install a skill without explicit Tomas approval.
- Do not encode secrets, private data, wallet details, or exchange/account details.
- A skill must state trigger, inputs, outputs, safety boundaries, and failure behavior.
